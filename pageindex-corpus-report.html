Systemic Issues

OCR Escalation Never Fires

Scanned/image-only documents produce <!-- image --> placeholders but escalated_ocr stays False. Zero text recovery on image-heavy PDFs. The pipeline silently persists hollow artifacts.

Affects 7 docs: MOU MOHRE, SLA, Cabinet Res 1, Cabinet Res 106, pie chart, UAE Numbers ×2

Tail-Blob Splitter (Fix-1)

Inline Article (N) markers not split by the line-anchored Latin regex. Largest blob: 319k chars (ﺣﻤﻮﺝ ﺍﻻﻨﺴﺎﻦ), 236k (Penal Code). Presentation-form Arabic (U+FExx) breaks the Arabic regex entirely.

Affects 6 docs: Penal Code (236k), ﺣﻤﻮﺝ ﺍﻻﻨﺴﺎﻦ (319k), Decree-Law 47 (11k), Decree-Law 33 EN (17k), Cabinet Res 21 (42k), Cabinet Res 96 (21k)

Garble-Gate Hole

validate_tree only checks structure (depth, node_count), not text integrity. Documents with corrupt text layers — numeric junk, mojibake from missing ToUnicode CMaps — pass validation and persist as "success".

Affects 3 docs: وارد 597 (91% digits), القرار التنظيمي (embedded font mojibake), مرسوم 13 (Latin mojibake)

Arabic OCR Text Corruption

Even when OCR fires (مرسوم 33), Arabic text shows character-level corruption: في→#, garbled article titles. Tessdata language selection or model quality issue.

Affects 2 docs: مرسوم 33 (في→# ×166), مرسوم 13 ("Oleg", "Nest" artifacts)

Table Column Structure

Complex table extraction degrades column-level structure: headers and values concatenated into single strings instead of separate cells. Data is present but requires string parsing.

Affects 3 docs: World Stats Pocketbook, GHV-TKV-Tarif, Unfallversicherung
Key Findings

German corpus is regression-free. All 27 documents pass or are marginal with only hierarchy-depth concerns — zero text quality issues, zero garbling. The flat-doc success route (RFC-004) correctly handles IPID/catalog documents.

English legal documents work well. Text-layer PDFs with standard encoding extract cleanly. Decree-Law copies produce consistent results. Tail-blobs only appear in very long documents with inline article markers.

Arabic is the primary failure domain. 11/13 FAILs are Arabic documents. Root causes: scanned-without-OCR (5), garble-gate bypass (3), presentation-form encoding (1), mojibake (2).

مرسوم 33 tail-blob fix validated. The most significant improvement: max_leaf dropped from 114k to 6.4k chars, with 72/74 articles individually split. Arabic article markers do work when text is in logical-form Unicode.

Pipeline never errors. All 62 documents completed with status "done" — zero timeouts, zero crashes. The issue is quality, not stability. Hollow artifacts are silently persisted as successes.

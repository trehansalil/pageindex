## Summary

All 7 architecture defect zones are **stalled**: 64 total bugs prior vs. 64 total bugs current, zero improved, zero regressed, zero new zones, zero closed zones (net bug delta: 0). Five of the seven zones now report their proposed refactor as `implemented_and_wired` (Zone 2 OCR/picture-plane, Zone 4 dual-pipeline candidates, Zone 5 reason/routing) or `partially_implemented` (Zone 1 verdict engine, Zone 3 RTL/garble) — meaning the typed scaffolding proposed in earlier remediation work has landed in production and is covered by tests — yet the bug counts have not moved for any of them, because in every case the underlying structural defect (dual pipelines, first-match cascade, overloaded routing variable) was left in place alongside the new types rather than replaced by them. Two zones (persistence writers, flag/threshold sprawl) still have no simplification proposal at all. The one substantive change this run is a single new finding in the flag-sprawl zone (`inspector_class` widening `cat_c` 1.2x with no flag guard, violating a worker.py timeout invariant), which was offset by consolidating two prior findings into one, leaving that zone's bug count unchanged at 7. Notably, MEMORY.md records Zone 5 (reason-as-routing-command) as closed via commit `db2e2f1`, but this audit still shows it open at critical severity with 8 bugs and identical findings — indicating the landed fix was partial, or ran under `first_defect`/`Route` scaffolding that didn't remove the old `reason` variable and its route-override branches (client.py:1249, 1548/1617/1643/1758).

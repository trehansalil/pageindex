Assessment

Iteration 2 caught two critical runtime defects (GB-1 would crash, GB-2 would silently lose Surya text) that survived iteration 1. Both stem from the same root: iteration 1 corrected the what (gate after pic_results, not md_content) but missed the how (TypedDict API) and the where precisely (before splice, not just after construction). All 8 findings resolved. The RFC-048 triad is now internally consistent, with all requirements mapped to design properties mapped to tasks with acceptance criteria.

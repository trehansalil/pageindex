2026-09-09 · three lenses (root-cause validation · implementation feasibility · lead analysis) · outcome: removal set unchanged at 59; plan amended

Verdict

The RFC's claims hold up almost everywhere — every one of the 59 names exists, the counts reconcile, the measurement reproduces exactly, and the simulated shrink empties precisely the two import lines it says it will. What did not hold up is the plan: one wave was self-contradictory, and two tools the plan depends on had never been written.

The lens judgement on the RFC itself: ready to execute, and over-deliberated by roughly two amendments. Further prose does not reduce the one residual risk — a consumer nobody scanned — while execution does.

MEDIUM · The metrics deferral argued against itself

An argument that clears the worker half cannot also justify deferring the metrics half. Compounding it: the RFC said wave 7, the tasks said wave 8, and wave 7 is the verification wave.

The steelman against executing at all

The shrink has zero runtime benefit; the only payoff is ending an audit loop. The D5 guard already freezes 412 entries and pins external consumers, so regrowth is solved without deleting anything. The package is never published to an index — but "never published" is not "never imported": a notebook, a cron container, or a Scaleway-side script copying this source the way services/docling-service does would fail only at runtime, invisibly to this suite. And open question 4 proved the scanners have already missed a broken reference once.

"Land the guard, skip the removal" is therefore a defensible terminal state — but only if the 59 are also recorded as accepted-dead in the manifest and the dead-code lens is taught to skip them. Otherwise the loop this RFC exists to end simply continues, which was the whole motivation. Weighed against a ~12–16h cost that is behaviour-neutral by simulation and revertable per package, execution wins. The narrowing that would have made sense is not "skip removal" but "skip converters" (26 of 59, the only real external contract) — and the docling-service pins plus TestConsumerReferencesResolve cover precisely that exposure.
